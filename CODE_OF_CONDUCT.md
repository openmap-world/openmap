# Code of conduct

OpenMap is a small community project; we'd like it to stay a friendly one.

- Be kind and assume good faith. Critique work, not people.
- No harassment, insults, slurs, or sexual or violent content, in any project space
  (issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent the project).
- Respect people's time and privacy: don't share others' personal information.
- Stay on topic, and accept a maintainer's decision on what fits the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who don't follow
this. To report a problem privately, use the repository's **Security** tab → **Report a
vulnerability** (it reaches the maintainers privately; it's fine to use it for conduct
reports too).

This follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
